## 4 · Calls longer than the 30 s API limit

A 77.8 s call (14 consecutive lines) split at pauses into 4 chunks (cuts inside a pause: 3/3). Prisma v3.0 kept **96%** of 25 amounts and dates, vs 96.4% line by line. WER 41.2% vs 38.5% (context lost at chunk edges). One call: a smoke test, not a statistic.

## 5 · What the real-call check broke

- The health check's dropout detector **does not work on real calls** (AUC 0.521 on 24 annotated dropouts vs 36 clean lines). It looks for digital silence; real networks conceal lost packets instead.
- So the simulated `loss_5` (zero-filled frames) is **optimistic**: real packet loss arrives as concealed, smeared audio, not silence.
- Real-call SNR (median clean line) matches the simulated phone condition within about 1 dB.
